Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-18

This Privacy Policy applies to the JustRelax mobile application (the “Application”) created and provided by Mustafa Kocer (the “Service Provider”). It explains what leaves your device when you use the Application, why, and what stays on your device.

Summary

  • No account or registration. No ads, and no advertising ID.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) are sent only if you allow them, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings.
  • If you use the AI mix, your text goes to OpenAI, which may use it to improve its models; the Application asks before your first mix. If you search for sounds online, your search goes to Freesound.
  • Sounds you import from your own files never leave your device.
  • The Service Provider does not sell your data and keeps no personal data on its own servers.

Details

AI mix. To create a mix, the Application sends the sentence you write, together with the names, categories and tags of your downloaded sounds, to OpenAI’s API. Your sentence may contain personal information if you choose to include it, so please do not write sensitive information. The Service Provider’s server passes the request on and does not store it. The Service Provider takes part in OpenAI’s data sharing program, so OpenAI may keep requests and responses and use them to develop and improve its models, under its own terms. The Application tells you this and asks for your agreement before your first AI mix.

Online sounds (Freesound). When you search for sounds, your search text is sent to Freesound through the Service Provider’s server, and previews and downloads come from Freesound’s servers. Only sounds under Creative Commons licenses that allow this use are shown, and their authors are credited in the Application.

Installation ID and Play Integrity. When the Application is installed, it creates a random ID that belongs only to this installation and is not linked to you. It is sent with AI and search requests so that the Service Provider’s server can limit how many requests one installation makes. On Android, Google Play Integrity confirms that the request comes from the genuine Application. These are used only to prevent abuse. Clearing the Application’s data or reinstalling it creates a new ID.

Server logs. The Service Provider’s server runs on Cloudflare Workers. It keeps no database of users or requests; its request limits are counters that expire after 60 seconds. Cloudflare’s logs, which include the request path (and so the search text), are kept for at most 3 days. Like any online service, Cloudflare, OpenAI, Freesound and Google receive technical information needed to answer a request, such as your IP address and the time.

Usage statistics and crash reports. On first launch the Application asks whether it may send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase. If you decline, nothing is sent. If you allow it:

  • Firebase Analytics receives events such as which screens are opened, which catalog sound is played and its category, how many sounds a saved mix has, whether an AI request or search succeeded and the type of any error, how many search results were found, the type of an imported sound, and the range of a sleep timer’s length, together with a Firebase app instance ID. It does not receive what you type, search or import.
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports and caught errors with the stack trace, device model and operating system version.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Usage data and crash reports. Analytics data is kept by Google for at most 14 months. At the time of writing these services are used only in the Android version.

Tips. Where the Application offers optional tips, they are one-time purchases processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The Service Provider receives only a confirmation that the purchase went through; it never sees your payment details. A tip unlocks nothing.

What stays on your device

  • Sounds you import from your files stay in the Application’s private storage. They are never uploaded and are not included in device backups.
  • Saved mixes, your library and downloaded sounds are kept in the Application’s storage on your device.
  • Settings (theme, language, your usage data choice) are stored on the device; on Android they may be included in your Google device backup, which is the only Application data that is.

The Application does not use your location, microphone, camera, contacts or photos.

These providers process data on the Service Provider’s behalf or to provide the feature you asked for, and may do so outside your country. OpenAI may also use AI mix requests to improve its own models, as described above. The Service Provider does not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and similar laws)

  • Usage statistics and crash reports: your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings.
  • AI mix: your consent, given in the notice before your first mix; you can withdraw it by no longer using the AI mix.
  • Online search: providing the feature you asked for.
  • Installation ID and Play Integrity: the legitimate interest of preventing abuse of the service.

Your choices

  • Decline or turn off usage statistics and crash reports in Settings.
  • Do not use the AI mix or the online search if you do not want text sent to OpenAI or Freesound.
  • Clear the Application’s data in your device settings, or uninstall it, to remove everything stored on the device.
  • Contact the Service Provider with any question or request about your data.

Security

All connections use encrypted HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but the Service Provider takes reasonable measures to protect the information the Application handles.

Children’s privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Application, please get in touch.
